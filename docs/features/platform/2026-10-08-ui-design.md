# The zenryx UI: from the contract to the datapoints (PLT-4, the web UI)

**Date.** 8 October 2026, 10:51. Asked for by the engineer at 10:38; written by Claude.

**Status.** Designed and built on 8 October (10:38-11:50), at the engineer's request ("plan the entire UI … and then build it"). It works locally. In PowerShell: `cd web`, then `npm.cmd ci` (on a fresh clone only), then `npm.cmd run dev`, then open http://localhost:5190. Nothing committed. Where it stands, and what is open: the [Platform page](status.md).

**What the engineer asked for, in short:**
- A UI built as the end-to-end product, not as a viewer of the current prototype's pictures. A trial made only to test something (the first variant) is not part of the product's flow, so it does not appear.
- It starts from the contract. Then it shows how each contract leads into campaigns, hypotheses, designs and grades, the agent's reasoning and next step, and how all of that becomes datapoints: decisions, the engineer's choices, counterfactual pairs, the four axes (task, attempt, evaluation, result).
- fastcae's UI is the model to copy: its design and its way of showing a pipeline.
- What exists now (the contracts) is shown. What the agent and the judge will make is laid out and left blank, to be filled when they are built.

---

## 1. Principles, carried over from fastcae's UI

1. **An engineering sheet, not a SaaS console.** Hairline rules, a quiet ground, white sheets, one blue; IBM Plex Sans and Mono; numbers in tabular mono. The tokens are copied from `fastcae-v1:ui/src/styles/tokens.css`.
2. **Every stage is shown, built or not.** A shell that hides its unbuilt stages describes a tool; one that shows them describes a product, and tells anyone looking where the work leads. A stage not built yet says so, and names what will fill it.
3. **Evidence is carried by line pattern as well as colour,** so it survives greyscale, screenshots and colour blindness. Here that is provenance: published, standard, measured, derived, assumption, engineer.
4. **Product UI, not documentation.** A few words an item, pills and toggles; the full text on hover or in the card on the right.
5. **Nothing on screen comes from anything but the records.** The UI reads one snapshot built from the repository's contracts. No number is typed into the UI.

## 2. The layout (fastcae's shell)

```
┌ topbar: mark · ZenryxAI · zenryx · the contract in focus · [Flow][Contracts][Campaigns][Designs][Data] ┐
├ rail ───────────┬ stage: its tabs, then the page ──────────────────────────┬ card ──────────────┤
│ what this tab   │                                                          │ whatever is in      │
│ lists, in the   │                                                          │ focus, in full:     │
│ order the work  │                                                          │ an entry, a step,   │
│ happens         │                                                          │ a design            │
├ titleblock: the snapshot's hash · zenryx version · contract schema · the counts ──────────────────┤
```

- The rail and the card are resizable and remember their widths; the card folds to a strip (copied: `Splitter`, `pane-strip`).
- **A tab may have no rail and no card** (the contract page); it then takes the full width. A tab with one page shows no page tabs.
- The address bar holds where you are (`#/contracts`, `#/data/-/tasks`), so any view can be linked or reopened.

## 3. The five tabs, in the order the work runs (since 8 October 20:35)

The engineer: "build it like this", after the walkthrough Contract → Campaigns → Designs → Data, with Flow summarising. Each tab is one page, with no rail and no card. The product opens on the Contract, and the bar names the contract on every tab.

| Tab | What it is for | Live now | Blank until |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Contract** (§4) | You give the inputs; the tools work out the rest; what a design must meet | everything | the planned calculations (GBX-1, the judge) |
| **Campaigns** (§5) | The agent's attempts: a new-campaign form, then its steps | the form's choices (solver, budget from the contract, stop rules); Start says what it waits for | the agent (AGT-1), and the contract frozen first |
| **Designs** (§6) | Every housing made: its stages, grade and rung, against the design to beat | the design to beat; the published mass on the chart; the rungs | geometry (GEO-1), the judge (PHY-1) |
| **Data** (§7) | What the record becomes: datapoints A-E, the cuts from campaigns, the tasks | the kinds and their examples in words; the 20 task types with their graders (2 ready) | the record (DAT-1 onwards) |
| **Flow** (§8) | Where the factory stands: one strip of steps, and what to build next | the counts; what is built | - |

The first version's tabs (a rail and a card each, several pages) are in `web/archive/*-v1/`, and described in [2026-10-08-ui-views-v1.md](../../archive/2026-10-08-ui-views-v1.md).

## 4. How the contract is shown: one product page (PLT-6, since 8 October 19:52)

The engineer asked for a product page, not documentation, with one contract. The page shows the first contract (Ivanov 2018) with no rail and no card, as one column. The first version's five pages are in the archive: [2026-10-08-ui-views-v1.md](../../archive/2026-10-08-ui-views-v1.md).

Top to bottom:
1. **The head:** the name, the family and level, the stage ladder (posed → derived → frozen), the checks passed, and the next step.
2. **Inputs:** what the user gives, in cards.
   - Duty; packaging, with the shaft angle the offset gives beside it; the gear train, a column per stage; bearings; process and material.
   - Each value sits in its box, with its source as a short label: "Ivanov 2018", "Shigley", "assumed", "you", "calculated". The full citation is on hover.
   - The process shows as a pre-selected choice.
3. **Calculations:** what the tools work out from the inputs.
   - Each shows its method as a pre-selected choice with its authors or standard in brackets, and the function that runs it.
   - Computed now: the gear mesh check, the ratio, the shaft angle, the shaft speeds, the mesh frequencies.
   - Planned, naming the tool that will make them: gear forces, shaft spacing, bearing loads, bearing sizes (GBX-1), and the alignment limit (the judge).
4. **Pass criteria:** each with its standard.
5. **The design to beat:** the published numbers. A published mode sitting on a mesh frequency at a check speed is flagged.
6. **The assumptions,** folded: what is still to confirm.

Everything on the page comes from `zenryx.api.contract_page`, so the UI types nothing in.

## 5. Campaigns

A campaign is one attempt at the frozen contract by one solver, with a budget.
- **New campaign:** the contract (it links back); the solver, a pre-selected choice (agent, rule-based workflow, optimiser, person); the budget from the contract (16 FE runs, 4 h); the stop rules as chips.
- **Start campaign** is disabled. It says what it waits for: the contract frozen (the load tool, GBX-1), then the agent (AGT-1).
- **Steps:** a step a row, read left to right: step · saw · tried, and why · tools · design · grade · next. Until a campaign runs, one row in words says what each column will hold. Every step becomes a decision record (C).

## 6. Designs

- **The design to beat:** the published numbers. Its mode on stage 2's mesh frequency is flagged.
- **Designs:** a design a row, with a dot for each stage (core, structure, join, castability, mesh, statics, modes), then the verdict, the mass and the rung. A row in words until one exists.
- **Mass against margin:** the frame every design will sit in. The published mass is a line to come in under, and the pass line sits at zero margin.
- **Rungs:** the five, the first two the platform reaches on its own.

## 7. Data

- **Datapoints:** the kinds A-E: what each is, an example from the contract in words (never numbers), what it trains, and its count.
- **Cut from campaigns:** decision records, snapshot tasks, preference pairs, with their counts.
- **Tasks:** the 20 task types in one shape, "given · hand in · graded by", worded for any contract. Each grader shows whether it exists: two are ready, through the validator's rules. Each type shows how many tasks of it have been made.

## 8. Flow

- **The factory:** one strip. Contract → derived → frozen task → campaigns → designs → grades → datapoints → tasks. Each step has its count and what makes it, solid when that is built and dashed when not; a click opens its tab.
- **Next to build:** GBX-1, PHY-1, GEO-1 and AGT-1 in order, each with what it unlocks.

## 9. Where the data come from

- `src/zenryx/api/snapshot.py` reads the product's contract, the first: Ivanov 2018, from `checks/data/contracts/`. It validates the contract, tries freezing it, builds its page (`zenryx.api.contract_page`), and writes everything the UI shows to `web/src/records/snapshot.json`: `uv run python -m zenryx.api.snapshot`.
- The snapshot is deterministic (no timestamp) and committed. A check fails when it is stale, as for the example contracts.
- The records the agent and the judge will make (campaigns, episodes, candidates, evaluations, decisions, the engineer's choices, pairs) are empty lists. Their shapes in `web/src/records/types.ts` are provisional until the record schema (DAT-1) is agreed.
- **No server now.** The UI bundles the snapshot. PLT-5 (the API) will serve the same payload; the UI's `loadSnapshot()` then fetches it instead.

## 10. Files

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `src/zenryx/api/snapshot.py` | The snapshot: the contract with its entries, checks, freeze readiness, sources and page; empty records |

| `web/src/styles/` | Copied from `fastcae-v1:ui/src/styles/` (tokens, shell, pipeline, designs), each with its origin header; `page.css`, every tab's page |
| `web/src/app/` | The shell, the tabs (`product.ts`), the route in the address bar |
| `web/src/contracts/`, `campaigns/`, `designs/`, `data/`, `flow/` | One page a tab |
| `web/src/panel/page.tsx` | The pages' pieces: the head, sections, values, sources, methods, badges |
| `web/src/words/` | The words a page uses: datapoint kinds, the 20 task types and their graders, stop rules and solvers, design stages and rungs |
| `web/src/records/` | `snapshot.json`, its types, `loadSnapshot()` |
| `web/archive/*-v1/` | The first version's tabs, outside the build, for reference |

## 11. Checks, and done when

- `uv run pytest checks -q` runs the snapshot's checks; ruff is clean.
- `npm run build` in `web/` passes: the types check and the bundle builds. CI runs it as a second job.
- Every tab looked at in a screenshot of the built page (headless Edge, no server).
- **Done when** the engineer runs `npm.cmd run dev` in `web/`, opens `http://localhost:5190`, and sees the contract through every tab, in the order the work runs, with every step not yet built visibly blank and naming what will fill it.

## 12. Not in this PBI

- The API server, and editing or freezing a contract from the UI (PLT-5 and later).
- The 3D viewer: copied from fastcae's renderer when designs and FE results exist (PLT-5, with GEO-1 and PHY-1).
- The first variant's pictures: a prototype, not a product record.
