Claude acts as that runtime agent for this housing (LOP-24): each move is a decision on the last result, logged here with its evidence so that the runtime agent's policy can be written from it and later tested against a scripted search ([the chained optimisation](../product/2026-10-11-chained-optimisation.md), §11, Test C).

The grade of a free run is the method's §4d: its lines in the optimiser's metal-or-air check (with the factor of safety), the paint (the shell's plan under members), the widths, the continuity, the recurrence, the metal placed. The baseline is `outcome_hfsdf_4mm_7.0kg` (the engineer, 20:16). Designs are named `it<n>_<what>_<kg>`.
