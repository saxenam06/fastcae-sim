No double factor, no fallback; a stiffness it cannot precondition fails, and says where its unrefined answer moves most.
  - **Read-out:** each seat's motion, each shaft's chord, the mounts' reactions against the loads, the mass, and 12 modes against the mesh frequencies at 6,000 rpm.
  - **Diagnostics:** `LOOP_STIFF="end:100,cover.side:10"` scales a region's stiffness (a ceiling, never a design); `LOOP_MODES=0`; `LOOP_LOADS=cosine` (D6a); `LOOP_CONTRACT` (a what-if contract).
- **The grade** (`grade.py`): the bearing tilt per seat and case is the seat's tilt against its shaft's chord **plus the shaft's own bend** there (`shafts.py`'s `lean_at_bearing`), against 0.75 × its type's limit. The seat's own rotation and the housing's share alone are printed beside it. The grade also checks the displacement against the reference's, the modes against the orders, and the mass.
- **Diagnosis:** `endwall.py` (the walls' bowing; each seat's wall and own shares; each chord split into the joint's share and the lobes'), from a stored answer.
- **Castability** (`castable.py <stem> …`, about 12 s a design, no FE). It checks the contract's casting rules on each half's CAD:
  - **the pull direction,** by two-sided visibility: each face turned along the pull or against it must see free space that way; a closed void counts as an undercut;
  - **the 4 mm least wall,** by a ray along each face's inward normal;
  - **the largest section,** from a 1 mm distance map (reported, not judged).

  It writes `castable_<stem>.json` and a picture: red is undercut, blue is under the least wall.
- **Pictures:** `poster.py` (one design: both sides, and sections through the shafts by displacement), `picture.py`, `deformed.py`.
- **The night's tools:** `topo.py` (voxel SIMP; compliance or the seats' tilt by an adjoint), `read.py` (fan and skeleton readings), `hypothesis.py`.
